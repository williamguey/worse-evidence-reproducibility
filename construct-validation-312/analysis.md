# Frozen analysis proposal

All results are model/configuration specific. The independent unit is a numerical case; its two presentation orders and all conditions remain inside one cluster. The plan has 312 fresh cases, shared by three configurations (Llama default, DeepSeek off, GPT-5.6 Luna off). The inferential target is the average expected contrast over the frozen numerical bank, conditional on that bank; fixed generator strata balance its composition; model configurations are not a sampled population. Independence across whole cases is an assumption. Shared-service dependence is not corrected by clustering or missingness bounds.

For case i, configuration m and condition c, let e_imco be the 0/1 wrong-report outcome in order o. The case contrast is the average of the two order-specific contrasts. The three directional primary endpoints per configuration are: (1) bundle_bad minus neutral_2; (2) bundle_bad minus bundle_good; (3) (bundle_bad minus neutral_2) minus (guard_bundle_bad minus guard_neutral). Their supports are [-1,1], [-1,1], [-2,2]. Nine planned tests form one Holm family at .05. Positive endpoint 3 means the cost-priority instruction attenuates the bundle contrast; it does not identify a psychological mechanism.

Inference uses a finite fixed betting mixture, not an exact sign-flip test. For support [-R,R] and null average E(X_i)<=mu, fix rho=(.1,.2,.4,.6,.8,1) and lambda=rho/(R+mu). Each product E_rho=product_i[1+lambda(X_i-mu)] is nonnegative. Independence yields E(E_rho)=product_i[1+lambda(E(X_i)-mu)] <= [1+lambda(average E(X_i)-mu)]^N <=1 by AM-GM, including nonidentical stratum means. The equal six-product mixture is therefore an E-value; p=min(1,1/E) is valid by Markov. Lambdas/weights are never selected from observed outcomes. Work in logs. Invert monotone mixture tests at alpha/2 for each tail to obtain two-sided confidence bounds; alpha=.05 nominal and .05/9 simultaneous for the nine primaries. Report signed effects and intervals even without rejection.

Unavailable errors remain in every planned case. Assign each missing positive-coefficient cell 0 and negative-coefficient cell 1 for its lower contrast L_i, and reverse assignments for U_i. Identification bounds are mean(L),mean(U). Primary tests use L and fixed support R: L_i<=X_i and the nonnegative fixed-mixture product is coordinate-monotone, so E(L)<=E(X) pointwise. Arbitrarily dependent missingness cannot inflate the test; a lower-envelope rejection establishes a positive full-plan contrast under independence of the full response clusters. CIs use the lower tail bound for L and upper tail bound for U. Complete-case estimates are descriptive and never replace the fixed-plan analysis. Missingness bounds do not impute actual responses or fix dependence.

Components, paraphrase, guard residual, simplification, neutral repeats and regret are secondary, with nominal CIs and no confirmatory significance claims. Regret is the audited gap times error; its betting range uses the deterministic generator bound 32.5, never the observed maximum gap. The stability diagnostic selects cases solely when both neutral_1 orders were correct and compares bundle_bad with the independent neutral_2 calls; it describes that selected sample, not internal certainty or a new population result. Controls remain descriptive validation counts and full-plan missingness bounds.

Outcome-free simulation records first-Holm-threshold power, order correlation, discordance, nonidentical means, and asymmetric lattice nulls. Ordinary t diagnostics were inflated under rare asymmetric weak nulls and are not confirmatory. Finite-mixture validity follows from the proof, not the finite simulation grid. Reference 20 pp guard-interaction power is checked prospectively at this case count; it is not a guarantee across nuisance scenarios. No scoring or scientific tuning occurs before genuinely terminal, drained collection.
